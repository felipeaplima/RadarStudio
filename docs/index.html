Frequently asked questions

How does activation work?

After you buy, you get a code by email. Open RadarStudio, enter the code on the activation screen and you're set — it's tied to that computer.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once activated, the app runs without internet for several days; it revalidates your license automatically when you're back online.

What equipment do I need?

A Windows computer and a Pocket Radar Smart Coach. You can connect by USB cable, Bluetooth or network.

How many computers can I use?

Each license works on up to 2 computers — handy when you use one PC at the radar and another for scouting. Need more machines? Just add another license.

What about renewal?

The license lasts 1 year. Near expiry we'll remind you to renew and keep the app running.

I changed computers, now what?

No problem — just contact us and we'll release activation on the new machine.
